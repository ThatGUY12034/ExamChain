Receive encrypted paper

Load the .enc file received from the university. Its hash is checked against the blockchain and the download is logged.

Decrypt at exam time

After release, the key is read from the blockchain. The decrypted paper is re-hashed to prove it was not tampered with.

Report a suspected leak

Found a "leaked paper" on WhatsApp or Telegram before the exam? Upload it. If its hash matches the committed paper, the leak is proven on-chain with a timestamp and the exam is marked compromised.

Immutable audit trail

Every action ever taken, read directly from blockchain events. Nobody, not even the admin, can edit or delete these.
